# Stage 12 — Community & governance

**Status:** In progress · **Depends on:** 02 (can run in parallel with 03–11) · **Branch:** `stage/12-community` · **Effort:** S

## Goal
Make it easy and safe for outside contributors, human or AI-assisted, to help (DEC-12). Make the project's
processes (decisions, roadmap upkeep, security reports) visible and repeatable.

## Why
- Community & governance scored 1/10 at baseline.
- With contributors, the rules in AGENTS.md (no BIOS files, non-destructive behavior, no silent tools) need a
  human-facing counterpart, and bug reports need the right information (Recalbox version, redacted `--json`
  output).

## Decisions applied
DEC-12, DEC-14, DEC-16, DEC-31, DEC-38, DEC-39, DEC-43.

## Stage acceptance criteria
- A contributor can find how to set up, what to work on, how to submit, and how to behave, without asking.
- Bug reports arrive with the version info and redacted JSON needed to act on them.
- Security reports have a private channel.

## Out of scope
- Funding, sponsorship, citation files and a docs site (not requested).

## Agent notes

  them in yourself.
